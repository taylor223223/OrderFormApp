Importing past orders from the company
  1. One row per item ordered (a line-item / invoice-detail export), not one row per invoice. Totals alone can't tell you colors or sizes.
  2. Include the unit # if the export has it. Rows with a unit become that unit's on-file sizes, so "unit 104" fills in next time. Rows without a unit are still saved to Purchase History.
  3. Excel or CSV both work, including report-style files with title lines at the top. The app finds the header row itself.
  4. Sizes and colors written in a description ("Vert blind 96 x 84 alabaster") are picked out automatically.
  5. Big file covering many properties? That's fine. Rows are matched to properties by acct # or name. Unmatched names become new customers on the review screen, where you can uncheck them.
The template shows the ideal layout. Your company's column names don't have to match; you'll map them on the next screen.
What the app does with it
  • Matches rows to customers by acct #, email, or name (ignores "Apartments", "The", etc.).
  • Fills blanks on existing customers (pre-checked) and flags conflicts (unchecked - you choose).
  • Adds new contacts, floorplans, units, and per-unit / per-floorplan measurements (skips ones already saved).
  • Rows with a PO, invoice # or date become previous orders. They show on each customer's Purchase History tab and power the smart dropdowns.
Recognised headers include: Property, Acct #, Address, City, State, Zip, Mgmt Co, Phone, Email, Contact, Unit, Building, Floorplan, Product, Room, Qty, Item #, Description, Width, Height, Color, Style, Core, Swing, PO, Invoice #, Date, Price, Amount, Notes. You can fix the mapping on the next screen.
