Import CSV / Excel

Previous orders, customer lists, unit lists or measurement sheets. Nothing is changed until you approve it on the review screen.
What the app does with it
  • Matches rows to customers by acct #, email, or name (ignores "Apartments", "The", etc.).
  • Fills blanks on existing customers (pre-checked) and flags conflicts (unchecked - you choose).
  • Adds new contacts, floorplans, units, and per-unit / per-floorplan measurements (skips ones already saved).
  • Rows with a PO or date become previous orders, which power the smart dropdowns.
Recognised headers include: Property, Acct #, Address, City, State, Zip, Mgmt Co, Phone, Email, Contact, Unit, Building, Floorplan, Product, Room, Qty, Item #, Width, Height, Color, Style, Core, Swing, PO, Date, Notes. You can fix the mapping on the next screen.
